How this is measured

The uploader reads the combat log for Kith'ix pulls on any difficulty, matching abilities by name. Checked against 32 Heroic pulls (Oct 2026); Mythic-only details haven't been seen in a log yet. The log doesn't say who touched a beetle: the name is whoever stood closest when it blew up. The room image is from raidstrats.gg and isn't lined up with the log's coordinates yet, so positions are approximate.
